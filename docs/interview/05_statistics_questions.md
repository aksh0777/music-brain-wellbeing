# Interview Questions — 05: Statistics Foundations

### 1. Basic Questions
* **Q: What is a $p$-value?**
  * **A**: A $p$-value is the probability of obtaining test results at least as extreme as the observed results, assuming that the null hypothesis ($H_0$) is true.

### 2. Citi-Style Practical Questions
* **Q: Why does correlation not imply causation?**
  * **A**: Correlation merely quantifies linear co-variation between two variables ($X$ and $Y$). It cannot rule out confounding variables ($Z$ causing both $X$ and $Y$) or reverse causality ($Y$ causing $X$).

### 3. Project Questions
* **Q: How do we apply hypothesis testing in our project?**
  * **A**: We test whether self-reported wellbeing scores significantly differ across listening habit groups using two-sample t-tests / Mann-Whitney U tests.
