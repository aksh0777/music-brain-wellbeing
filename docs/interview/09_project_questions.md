# Interview Questions — 09: Project Specific Defense

### 1. High-Level Defense Questions
* **Q: Why did you build "Music, Brain & Wellbeing"?**
  * **A**: I built this to extend audio mood classification into human-centric data science. I wanted to analyze how music listening habits relate to psychological wellbeing and evaluate whether physiological biosignals (EEG/ECG/GSR) provide incremental predictive power.

* **Q: What are the main limitations of your project?**
  * **A**:
    1. Behavioral listening data is observational; we can prove correlation and predictive power, but not causal influence.
    2. Self-reported wellbeing survey scores carry subjective reporting noise.
    3. Biosignal recordings require careful artifact cleaning and cannot be trivially combined across non-overlapping subject sets.
