# Interview Questions — 10: Behavioral & System Design

### 1. Behavioral STAR Questions
* **Q: Tell me about a time you faced a technical disagreement during a data science project.**
  * **A**: Focus on data leakage or model metric selection. Explain the Situation, Task, Action (using empirical evidence/benchmarks), and Result (achieved audited accuracy and team alignment).

* **Q: How do you handle messy, missing, or uncalibrated real-world data?**
  * **A**: Explain systematic data integrity validation: checking distribution shifts, missingness mechanisms (MCAR vs MAR vs MNAR), domain-appropriate imputation, and recording every decision in an empirical decision log.
