# Interview Questions — 06: Machine Learning Foundations

### 1. Basic Questions
* **Q: What is the bias-variance tradeoff?**
  * **A**: High bias occurs when a model is overly simplistic and underfits the training data. High variance occurs when a model is overly complex and overfits to noise in the training data. The goal is to minimize total generalization error.

### 2. Citi-Style Practical Questions
* **Q: Why use Logistic Regression as a baseline model instead of jumping straight to XGBoost?**
  * **A**: Logistic Regression provides a linear, interpretable benchmark with probabilistic outputs. It establishes a simple performance floor to justify the added complexity and computational cost of non-linear ensemble models.
